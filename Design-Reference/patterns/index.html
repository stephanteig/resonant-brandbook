Patterns & flows

The product keeps the listening thread.

Patterns describe how named components work together. They capture intent, progression, decision points, and recovery—not just a collection of screens.

01 / SEARCH

Search stays in context.

Make the source visible, preserve the query, and let a person move from result to listening without losing their place.

FindSearch Spotify
InspectArtist, album, track
ListenPlay or open context
KeepSave to your music
02 / PLAYLIST REVIEW

Propose before mutating.

Playlist changes are reviewable decisions. The pattern makes scope, reasoning, confidence, and the effect on Spotify explicit.

01

Inspect

Show playlist source, freshness, and scope.

02

Explain

Name the finding in human language.

03

Decide

Keep, remove, or mark unsure.

04

Apply

Confirm before creating or changing in Spotify.

03 / DISCOVERY

Surprise needs a frame.

Discovery has levels of familiarity. Every recommendation carries a reason, a source, and a clear action.

SAFE PICK

A familiar return

Close to something already in your listening.

EXPLORE

A nearby turn

Connected by a pattern Resonant can explain.

WILDCARD

A real left turn

Unexpected, but never presented without context.

04 / SPOTIFY SYNC

Connection is explicit.

Spotify is the first music source, not Resonant’s identity. Show the boundary and the consequence of every connection state.

DISCONNECTED

Connect Spotify

Discover and Taste need authorised listening context.

CONNECTED

Spotify connected

Listening context is available. You remain in control.

CHECKING

Checking Spotify

Preserve the current screen while the provider responds.

05 / AI EXPLANATION

AI appears at the point of need.

It helps with a playlist, a taste observation, or a discovery. It never becomes a dominant blank chatbot surface.

06 / SETTINGS & ONBOARDING

Permission before personalisation.

Onboarding establishes the account, then connects Spotify separately. Settings make data, provider access, and sign-out understandable.

STEP 01

Create account

Sign in with Google to create your Resonant account.

STEP 02

Connect Spotify

Choose whether Resonant can access listening context.

STEP 03

Start listening

Open the Player, Search, Taste, or Discover with context.
