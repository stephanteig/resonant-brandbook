# Resonant Design System
## 04 — WRITING GUIDE

## Purpose

This guide defines the voice used in the web app, AI features, website, release notes, support material, and future Brand Book.

## Voice

Resonant is intelligent, calm, curious, honest, precise, and human. It speaks like a skilled collaborator who respects the maker’s decisions.

## Tone by situation

| Situation | Tone | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal action | Direct and quiet | “Open playlist.” |
| Progress | Specific and reassuring | “Updating your taste profile…” |
| Error | Factual and helpful | “Spotify could not be reached. Try again.” |
| Consent | Explicit and non-judgmental | “Spotify connects separately from your Resonant account.” |
| AI uncertainty | Honest | “This is a writing suggestion; listen and revise before generating.” |

## Rules

- Use verbs for actions: **Play**, **Save**, **Open**, **Connect Spotify**, **Analyze**, **Create playlist**.
- Use sentence case for explanatory copy; all caps may be reserved for compact labels already established in the UI.
- Name the object and consequence: “Disconnect Spotify? Discover and Taste will lose access to listening data.”
- Keep errors actionable; never blame the person.
- Do not imply that taste signals or recommendation levels are objective judgments.
- Do not claim AI output is unique, safe, or guaranteed to be copyrightable.

## Do / don’t

| Do | Don’t |
| --- | --- |
| “Connect Spotify to make Discover personal.” | “Upload your music to the cloud.” |
| “No project is open.” | “Oops! Nothing here yet!” |
| “The project changed outside Resonant. Reload it?” | “Sync conflict detected.” |
| “Connect Spotify to make Discover personal.” | “Unlock next-generation creativity.” |

## Accessibility

Avoid idioms, unexplained abbreviations, and sound-only instructions. Pair keyboard shortcuts with visible labels. Announce important status changes through the existing live region without flooding it.
