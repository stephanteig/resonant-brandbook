# 29 — MOBILE

## Purpose

This page prevents the unverified mobile idea from being mistaken for current product scope while preserving a place for future requirements.

## Status

No published mobile application or mobile companion is verified in the product repository. This page records constraints for future planning only.

## Future principles

Do not shrink every desktop surface into a phone. Choose a focused task, preserve listening context, respect touch targets, support interruption, and make Spotify/account status explicit.
