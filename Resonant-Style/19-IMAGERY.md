# 19 — IMAGERY

## Purpose

Imagery should make the act of listening and discovering music feel tangible without turning Resonant into an entertainment feed.

## Direction

Prefer dark, cinematic surfaces; close, honest views of listening; restrained glow; and real or cleared music artwork. The product’s visual energy comes from artwork, metadata, taste signals, and sound—not stock lifestyle scenes.

## Artwork and photography

Use artwork supplied by the maker or cleared for the intended use. Do not imply that generated imagery is product functionality. Photography should feel observed and specific; avoid generic headphones-on-a-desk stock clichés.

## Illustration

Illustration is supportive and geometric. It may explain context, relationships, discovery risk, or data boundaries, but must not invent shipped features.

## Accessibility and licensing

Add alt text, captions where required, sufficient contrast, and asset provenance. Never publish repository screenshots or third-party artwork in a marketing deliverable without confirming rights.

## Composition rules

Give one image one job: establish the product, explain a workflow, or add atmosphere. Keep controls and copy away from high-frequency artwork. Crop around musical action and use overlays only when contrast-tested.

## Illustration Required template

**Purpose:** explain a concept without inventing product UI.

**Description:** identify the object, relationship, and emotional temperature.

**Suggested composition:** specify focal point, crop, surface, and annotation placement.

**Priority:** P0/P1/P2.

**Assets required:** list approved screenshots, vectors, fonts, and rights.
