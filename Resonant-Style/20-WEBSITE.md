# 20 — WEBSITE

## Purpose

The public website introduces Resonant as a personal music control center and should feel like the product’s first editorial surface.

## Current landing-page direction

The verified landing page uses the message “Listen with context.” It introduces Why Resonant, the product areas, Account, and the principle “Music first. Intelligence second.” It is public, static, independent of Spotify and Supabase data, and must work without credentials.

## Recommended page system

```text
Hero → Why Resonant → Product areas → Taste / playlists / discovery story
     → Player context → Account / connect Spotify → closing message
```

The website should explain the relationship between listening, taste, playlists, Discover, Player, and Assistant without presenting planned capabilities as shipped. It should make the Spotify connection boundary clear before asking for access.

## Content hierarchy

1. What Resonant is.
2. Why context matters.
3. What Home, Search, Library, Playlists, Discover, Taste, Player, and Account do.
4. What requires Spotify connection.
5. How account and privacy boundaries work.

## Visual direction

Use large editorial type, quiet dark surfaces, warm gold signal colour, thin rules, generous margins, music artwork, and restrained UI crops. The web page should feel more like an invitation into a world than a technical feature matrix.

## Accessibility and responsive behaviour

Provide semantic landmarks, skip navigation, visible focus, reduced-motion support, clear anchor targets, and no horizontal overflow at 320 px. Do not hide the entire product story behind animation.

## Illustration Required

**Purpose:** show how the brand moves from public story to product surface.

**Description:** hero composition plus a sequence of real UI crops for Home, Player, Taste, Playlists, Discover, and Account.

**Suggested composition:** full-bleed dark hero with a warm signal line; alternating text and product fragments below.

**Priority:** P0. **Assets:** final landing screenshot, approved artwork, and cleared fixture data.
