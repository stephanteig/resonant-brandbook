# Resonant Design System
## 05 — BRAND IDENTITY

## Purpose

This chapter defines the identity of Resonant as a product and the language that should carry into the website, app, presentations, and documentation.

## Position

Resonant is a calmer way to live with music: a personal control center that brings listening, taste, playlists, and discovery together without flattening music into numbers.

## Mission

Help people understand the music they return to and make better discoveries from it.

## Vision

Become the most thoughtful place to listen with context.

## Brand promise

Resonant keeps the thread between what you listen to, what you save, what you notice, and what you discover next.

## Values

| Value | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Curiosity | Encourage exploration without turning it into endless scrolling. |
| Context | Connect songs, patterns, playlists, and recommendations meaningfully. |
| Honesty | Show estimates, sources, freshness, and uncertainty. |
| Craft | Make every surface considered, useful, and quiet enough for music. |
| Agency | Let the person decide what to keep, remove, correct, or ignore. |
| Respect | Keep account, provider, and personal listening data boundaries explicit. |

## Personality

Resonant is observant, warm, intelligent, editorial, and quietly confident. It is never loud, gamified, smug, or algorithmically overbearing.

## Messaging hierarchy

1. **Listen with context.**
2. **Your music is more than a history.**
3. **Your music, with context.**

Use one primary message per surface. Do not combine every tagline into one hero.

## What Resonant is / is not

| Is | Is not |
| --- | --- |
| A personal music control center | A social network |
| Context-first | A popularity dashboard |
| Explainable | A black-box recommendation feed |
| Music-led | A generic AI chatbot |
| Calm and editorial | A gamified listening app |
