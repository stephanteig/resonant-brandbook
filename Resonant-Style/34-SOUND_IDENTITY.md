# 34 — SOUND IDENTITY

## Purpose

Sound identity defines how Resonant uses audio, silence, and motion without interrupting listening.

## Principles

Music is never covered by interface sound. Any UI sound must be short, quiet, optional, and meaningful. Playback audio belongs to the user; interface feedback belongs to the product and must be distinguishable.

## Current status

No canonical Resonant sound logo, notification set, or interaction sound library is verified in the product repository. Do not present proposed sounds as part of the current product.

## Future system

Potential categories are confirmation, error, connection, and completion. Avoid notification sounds for ordinary navigation. Respect system mute and reduced sensory settings.

**Purpose:** explain sound identity without embedding audio in the PDF.

**Description:** visual timeline of a quiet confirmation, provider connection, and export completion.

**Suggested composition:** waveform fragments with duration, loudness, and use labels; no decorative musical notation.

**Priority:** P1. **Assets:** approved audio prototypes, loudness measurements, and usage matrix.
